# Productivity Planner 

A premium productivity dashboard designed to help you stay focused and productive. 

**[🔗 View Live Project](https://productivityplanner-eight.vercel.app/)**

## Features

- **To-Do List**: Capture everything on your mind and manage your tasks efficiently.
- **Daily Planner**: Schedule and organize your day.
- **Daily Quote**: Start your day right with a daily dose of motivation.
- **Pomodoro Timer**: Boost your focus and time management using the Pomodoro technique.
- **Daily Goals**: Set and track your key daily targets.
- **Local Weather**: Automatically fetches and displays your local weather. *(Requires location access).*
- **Theme Toggle**: Switch between light and dark modes for comfortable day or night viewing.

##  Usage Instructions

1. **Visit the dashboard**: Open the **[live link](https://productivityplanner-eight.vercel.app/)**.
2. **Enable Weather**: When opening the app, your browser may prompt you for location permissions. Click **Allow** so the app can detect your location and display the accurate local weather on the top right!
3. **Explore**: Click on the cards at the bottom (To-Do List, Daily Planner, etc.) to access each feature.
4. **Toggle Theme**: Use the moon/sun icon in the top right to change the visual theme.
